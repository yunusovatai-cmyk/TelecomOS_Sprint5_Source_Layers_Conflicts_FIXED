TELECOMOS SPRINT 3 — OBJECT REVIEW

1. Stop Sprint 2:

cd ~/Downloads/TelecomOS_Sprint2_KMZ_Import
docker compose down

2. Start Sprint 3:

cd ~/Downloads/TelecomOS_Sprint3_Object_Review
docker compose up --build

3. Open:

http://127.0.0.1:3000

4. Import your KMZ.
5. Open Engineering Map.
6. Click an object.
7. Add an engineering note.
8. Approve or Reject.
9. Press Refresh and confirm the status remains saved.
